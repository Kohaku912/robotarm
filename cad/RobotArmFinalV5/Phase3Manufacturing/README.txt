Robot Arm Final V5 Phase 3 Manufacturing

This directory contains unique printable PartDesign bodies, STEP and STL exports, and fit coupons.
Print the coupons before structural parts. Do not force bearings or heat-set inserts into an unvalidated fit.
P05/P06/P07 are quantity-two mirrored side plates. P16/P17/P18 are quantity-five modular parts.
The gear tooth profiles are generated with sampled 20-degree involute flanks and must be verified with C04/C05 before loading the arm.
Final material, layer orientation and fastener lengths remain subject to bench testing.
